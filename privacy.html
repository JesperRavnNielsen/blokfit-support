Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy version 1.0 for the BlokFit iOS app

The BlokFit iOS app does not collect personal user data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

Routines and settings stay on your device (and may sync to a paired Apple Watch). The only network use is Apple’s App Store / In‑App Purchase for unlocking BlokFit Pro.

It is just an interval timer.

If you wish to verify this for yourself, see the source code at https://github.com/JesperRavnNielsen/BlokFit
